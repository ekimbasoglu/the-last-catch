Support

The Last Catch: Cozy Fishing

Stuck on a fish, lost a save or found a bug? Write to ekimbasoglu@hotmail.com and say which device and iOS version you play on. I answer every email.

Quick help

  • Fishing: tap ACTION at the ripples to cast, tap when the float dips, then hold to keep the fish inside the green bar.
  • Saving: the game saves when you sleep and as you play. Three save slots are on the title screen.
  • Controls: touch controls by default; a game controller works too. Change the layout in the pause menu.
  • Ads: the game is free, with a short ad now and then while the screen fades between areas, never while you fish or shop. "Remove Ads" in the pause menu, on the title screen or at the end of a day turns them off for good.
  • Restore a purchase: pause menu → SUPPORT → RESTORE PURCHASE, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple; request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Ad privacy: pause menu → SUPPORT → AD PRIVACY (shown where consent applies). See the privacy policy.
  • Deleting your data: everything the game keeps is on your device; deleting the app removes it.
